The route at a glance

A three-week Perth loop from wine country of the Capes Region to the remote south coast.

Three nights in Perth to start, then south through Margaret River and the southern forests, east across the Great Southern to the Stirling Range and the Recherche Archipelago, and back inland from Esperance via Wave Rock. Some unsealed roads, 4WD tracks and drive-on beaches are included only if conditions and permissions allow.
